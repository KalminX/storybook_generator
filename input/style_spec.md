# Storybook Style Specification

> This is a placeholder. Replace it with your own style specification.
> Everything in this file is sent to the AI for every story and every illustration.

## Audience
Children aged 3–6, read aloud by a parent.

## Story
- 10–12 pages.
- 1–3 short, simple sentences per page.
- Warm, gentle tone with a clear, positive ending.
- Keep the same small cast of characters throughout.

## Illustration style
- Soft watercolor with gentle ink outlines.
- Warm, pastel color palette; bright but not saturated.
- Simple, uncluttered backgrounds.
- Characters are friendly and expressive, never scary.
- No text, letters, or words inside the illustrations.
